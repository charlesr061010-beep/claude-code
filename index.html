How to play

  1. Seven innings, seven prompts. For each one, name a single MLB player who fits.
  2. Go deep. Every correct answer scores, but rare answers fly farther. Your total is your distance in feet; 700 ft is perfect.
  3. Pitch clock: 30 seconds. Run out of time or name someone who doesn't fit and it's a strikeout. Need longer? Set the clock to 60 seconds or off before you start; results note it.
  4. One at-bat per player. You can't use the same player twice in a game.
  5. Daily or Unlimited. Everyone gets the same daily game, and its prompts don't repeat for months. Unlimited deals new games forever and can focus on one team.

Where rarity comes from

Moonshot doesn't have a crowd of past guesses yet, so each answer's rarity is an estimate. Every player gets a fame score from awards, All-Star games, the Hall of Fame, milestones and how recently he played. That score is weighted by how closely he fits the prompt (Babe Ruth fits "Yankees" far better than "Braves") and turned into an estimated share of fans who'd name him. A star on a brief detour, like Mike Piazza's five games as a Marlin, scores Too Cute.

A new daily game starts at midnight your time. Team prompts count the whole franchise: Expos seasons count for the Nationals, Brooklyn for the Dodgers. "With the team" means the honor came while he played there; "and won" means with any team.
